---

## Goals

- Start a retro or review in under a minute and share one code  
- Control section timing so the meeting finishes on schedule  
- Keep brainstorm psychologically safe, then surface themes fairly  
- Leave with owned actions or a clear release decision  
- Export a record for the team wiki without retyping  

## Frustrations

- Sticky-note tools that leak drafts before discuss  
- Tools that mix voting, notes, and actions on one noisy board  
- Scoring spreadsheets that disagree with the phone screenshot  
- Accounts and SSO friction for a 45-minute ritual  

## Behaviors in TeamCompass

- Creates the room; becomes `hostId`  
- Picks a shape via shelves/search (Classic → Food; 3/4/5 columns) and an icebreaker (including Health Check, Travel Wishlist, or Lineup); may customize This or That pairs or name a Lineup title  

- Advances phases; starts 3/5/8 minute timers (or Custom 1–60 on web)—timers are open to any joined person on web  
- Chooses discuss walk (status vs author), focuses groups, and uses the raise-hand queue to manage speaking  
- Adjusts vote budget on web when needed (open to joined actors; not host-only); drives action assignment  
- Opens Summary and downloads CSV and PPT (health answers export as Anonymous; travel as country names; lineup as ranked names)  
- Hosts planning or refinement checklists when the team needs a Forecast / Selectable call  
- May open a product direction room when the team needs a shared vision or strategy statement  
- Uses product requirements when a single requirement needs a Ready / Thin / Still open call  
- Points the team at artifact examples when onboarding language for Scrum / Agile / SAFe docs  

## Needs from the product

| Need | Product response |
| --- | --- |
| Host authority | Host-gated phase, icebreaker, discussion mode (timers/vote budget open on web) |
| Clear sections | Each retro phase shows only that section’s work |
| Timing | Per-section countdown + stopwatch (retro + checklist sections) |
| Trustworthy privacy | Server-side brainstorm filtering; Health Check peer anonymization |
| Closure | Actions + CSV/PPT; planning / refinement / review calls |

## Success looks like

A 60-minute retro ends with three owned actions, a PPT in Slack, and no one saying “my note showed too early.”
